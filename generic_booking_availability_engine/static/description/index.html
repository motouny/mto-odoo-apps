Bookable Resources • Date Availability • Seasonal Pricing • Cancellation Policies

A domain-agnostic booking engine for anything sold or rented by the day or by a date range — hotel rooms, vehicles, tour guides, equipment rental, clinic slots, event seats, meal plans — any bookable resource, entirely inside Odoo.

Highlights

  • Bookable resources organized by category, each with its own capacity, base price and cancellation policy
  • Day-by-day availability calendar with per-date capacity overrides and blackout dates
  • Seasonal / special-offer pricing rules resolved by priority and date range
  • Configurable cancellation policies (refund percentage by days-before-start)
  • A full booking workflow: draft, pending payment, payment failed, paid, pending supplier confirmation, confirmed, rejected by supplier, cancelled by customer, cancelled by admin, refund requested, refunded, completed, no-show
  • Automatic capacity guard — a booking cannot be confirmed past a resource's available capacity for its requested dates
  • Automatic refund percentage calculation from the resource's cancellation policy
  • Portal self-service: customers see only their own bookings, download the booking voucher and request cancellation
  • Printable booking voucher (PDF)
  • Bulk availability generator wizard
  • Multi-company, works standalone or alongside standard Sales/Invoicing
  • Arabic and English, right-to-left ready

This app is fully independent — it does not require or depend on any other paid application from this publisher.
